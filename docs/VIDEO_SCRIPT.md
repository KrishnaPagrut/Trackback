# Trackback: the demo film (the part after the skit)

A walkthrough of one context graph, in the order it was built: the company, the source document, the work piled on top of it layer by layer, the problem found in the source, and what Trackback does about it (the reconciliation, the pings, Jade, the people, the learning, the replay). It ends by sending the viewer to the live link, which opens in Deep mode on this same company with a tour.

Demo portion: 12 scenes, 164 seconds. The black-and-white skit (about 22 s) is cut in front of scene 1 and is not part of this brief. Every figure in the narration is on screen in its scene and comes from the canonical world (`data/snapshots/canonical.sqlite`), not invented.

## For /brag

Invocation: `/brag --voice --full --duration 165 --tone polished --format landscape --title "Trackback"`. Use this file as the storyboard: one scene per row, the narration verbatim, the listed frame as the real UI of that scene (frames are 1920x1080 captures of the running app in `docs/screenshots/round-6/`, re-shootable with `backend/scripts/design_shots.py 6` against `make demo`). Identity: paper #F7F4EE, ink #1C1B19, red #C62828 (only for the correction and refusals), blue #1F5FBF (pings, highlights), green #2E7D32; Newsreader for headlines, IBM Plex Sans for captions, IBM Plex Mono for every number (all bundled in `frontend/`). Captions on: judges watch muted. Motion should follow the story (a layer slides in when the narration names it; the path lights up when "click the certificate" is said), never decorate it. No music under scenes 8 and 9; bring it back for 12.

## The facts the narration uses

Six gyms in California; Q1 revenue $10.4M (about $40M a year); term loan, net debt $16.5M; TTM adjusted EBITDA $5.6M; net leverage 2.95x against a 3.00x covenant. Billing export v1 captured April 9, 2:02 pm; Q1 close April 10 (51 pieces of work by the Close Accountant, $236,000 March deferred-revenue release); board deck published April 24; certificate signed by the CFO and sent May 15; GM bonuses paid May 31; Q2 estimated tax paid June 15; correction July 8: $118,000 counted twice across five gyms; leverage 2.95x → 3.03x; 10 of 174 pieces of work needed an agent, 44 looked at, 4 actions waiting for a person; Jade overruled 2 of 4 calls; gate 71 of 72 past claims pass, 0 newly failing; held-out playbook v1 80.6% → v2 100%; 188 artifacts, 305 receipts at rest.
