Every change after the first commit of `answer_key.yaml`, with the reason. The engine is tuned to the key, not the other way round;
entries here are omissions or inconsistencies in the hand-written key itself.

The original value was a guess made before the classification rules were written down.
- (iteration 2). Reason vocabulary for prose. Prose whose cited figures did not move in display and whose conclusion holds is cleared deterministically as `conclusion_unchanged`; `prose_judged` now means an LLM (or the literal-substitution precheck) actually judged it. Entries changed: E02 and E20 `deck_s_covenant`, `cert_s_compliance` (prose_judged → rerendered; under E11 both stay prose_judged because the covenant conclusion flips and the owning agent is pinged); E10 `deck_s_covenant` (unaffected/prose_judged → invalidated/rerendered: "about $100k of headroom" is re-rendered to "about $86k"); E10 `cert_s_compliance`, E03 and E04 `cash_s_may` (prose_judged → conclusion_unchanged).
